# Changelog: Wishlist

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added
- Read a thing's link again: reply `read again` to it on WhatsApp (also `refresh`, `check price`), or use the button on the page. The page's name, picture and price replace what was read before; a price the person typed stays.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added
- First release, unlisted. Send a link to something you want, or type it with its price, and it is kept on a list with its name, picture and price. `list` shows what is left to buy and what it adds up to; ✅ on a thing marks it bought.
- Several lists: `new list diwali` starts one in the same chat, `new group diwali` gives one a WhatsApp group of its own, and a group the person already had can hold a list too. A list's name before a link puts it there; a link on its own goes to the list used last in that chat.
- Replies to a thing set its price, labels, name or note, move it or delete it.
- Sharing: `share` gives a link to a page anyone can open, with the list and its prices and nothing about who made it. `stop sharing` closes the link for good.
- A page on app.whatodo.today: every list with its pictures and totals, adding by link or by words, editing, moving, sharing.
